## Data and metric interpretation

The supplied workbook is structurally consistent with UCI's 303-record/59-field extension, but local-file authenticity is not independently established. Its `Cath`/vessel labels contain one contradiction. A safe parser correction now maps `Cath` to CAD when CAD is absent and recognizes `Stenotic`; regression tests cover mappings and unknown-label rejection. The training loader and fixed predictor schema still do not support this workbook. Therefore no actual-workbook metrics are available; see `dataset_audit.md` and `ml_leakage_and_validation.md`. The current model metrics are synthetic only.
